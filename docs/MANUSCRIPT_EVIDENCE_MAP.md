## 1. Executive Summary & Evidence Governance Rules

This Evidence Map serves as the foundational, binding reference for drafting the TCGA-LIHC multi-omics preprint manuscript. In accordance with master instructions, every statement in the manuscript must map directly to an entry in this matrix.

### Governance Directives:
1. **Absolute Source Rule:** No study-specific fact may be written from memory, guesswork, or generic knowledge. Unverified values are flagged `[DATA NOT VERIFIED]` and halted.
2. **Conservative Tone:** The manuscript reports an *original model-development study* demonstrating *moderate discrimination* ($C \approx 0.59\text{--}0.62$). All marketing rhetoric ("breakthrough", "superior", "state-of-the-art", "first-ever", "clinically ready") is strictly prohibited.
3. **No External Validation Claims:** The $N=65$ test set is a *locked internal holdout* (partitioned from TCGA-LIHC before analysis). External validation in an independent geographic/clinical cohort was not performed and is explicitly acknowledged as a future requirement.
4. **Primary Model Identity:** ElasticNet-Cox ($\alpha = 0.5$) was selected for grouping correlated latent factors and numerical stability. LASSO-Cox achieved a marginally higher mean cross-validation C-index ($0.5910$ vs $0.5875$); therefore, ElasticNet is **never** termed the "best-performing" model.
5. **No Invalid Holdout Metrics:** The legacy value $C = 0.6696$ (an artifact of un-split feature selection / leaky projection) is completely eliminated. The authoritative locked holdout C-index is **$0.6197$** ($95\%\text{ CI: } 0.4785\text{--}0.7405$).
6. **Clinical Incremental Value:** Multi-omics alone ($C = 0.5875$) does not surpass clinical staging alone ($C = 0.5974$). Combining them yields a modest, non-conclusive increment ($\Delta C = +0.0146, p = 0.272$). Multi-omics provides complementary signal but cannot replace AJCC staging.
7. **Biological Interpretation:** Latent Factor 2 captures a robust transcriptomic contrast between hepatocyte differentiation/metabolic programs and mitotic cell-cycle progression. All enrichment claims are strictly associative/correlative; causal claims ("drives", "causes", "proves mechanism") are banned.
